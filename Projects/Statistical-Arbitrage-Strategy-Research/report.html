Statistical Arbitrage Strategy Research

A complete pairs-trading research workflow covering data quality, cointegration screening, dynamic hedge-ratio estimation, signal design and strict out-of-sample validation.

Executive conclusion

REJECTED AT BASELINE. The strategy produced negative performance before and after transaction costs. The research therefore does not claim a profitable trading strategy; it documents why the apparent mean-reversion relationship failed to translate into robust out-of-sample alpha.

Research workflow

1 · DataCollected and quality-checked daily split-adjusted closing prices and volume data.
2 · Pair screeningUsed return correlation, Engle–Granger cointegration and mean-reversion diagnostics.
3 · Signal modelEstimated dynamic hedge ratios with a Kalman Filter and constructed Z-score signals.
4 · BacktestApplied delayed execution, daily rebalancing and transaction-cost sensitivity analysis.

Selected pairs and portfolio construction

CVX–COP

Energy-sector pair selected through the statistical screening process. The dynamic beta was updated daily to maintain a beta-normalized long/short spread.

PEP–GIS

Consumer-staples pair evaluated with the same signal and execution rules, providing a second independent portfolio sleeve.

Each pair received 50% of initial capital. Signals observed at close t were executed at close t+1, and the new position first earned return over the following close-to-close interval.

Why the strategy failed

  • Weak underlying alpha: the no-cost portfolio was already negative.
  • High turnover: daily hedge-ratio updates generated meaningful transaction costs.
  • Tail losses: four stopped trades generated the largest losses and none was profitable.
  • Mean-reversion trades were cost-sensitive: 33 regular exits earned approximately +6.46% gross, but about 6.61% of cost left them roughly flat.

Scope limitations

The dataset contains split-adjusted closing prices rather than dividend-reinvested total returns. Dividends, explicit short-borrow fees, taxes and market impact are not modeled. The next-close execution convention is deliberately conservative, and no Phase 4 parameter was optimized on out-of-sample profitability.
